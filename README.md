# FIN-DSP

## The Audio & DSP Engineering Organization

**FIN-DSP** is the audio, digital signal processing, music technology, and experimental sound engineering organization within the broader CTP ecosystem.

We build software and research projects for **Linux, CTP-EOS, MIDI, digital audio, synthesis, signal processing, experimental music, and next-generation creative computing.**

Our work sits at the intersection of:

* Digital Signal Processing
* Audio Software Engineering
* Music Technology
* MIDI & Hardware Integration
* Electronic Music
* Generative & Algorithmic Music
* Experimental Sound
* Linux Audio
* CTP-EOS
* Consciousness-oriented audio research

## What We Build

FIN-DSP develops and maintains software ranging from practical Linux audio utilities to experimental instruments, trackers, synthesizers, DSP systems, and research projects.

Our projects may include:

* Audio applications
* MIDI tools and sequencers
* Trackers
* Synthesizers
* Effects processors
* DSP libraries
* Audio plugins
* Signal-analysis tools
* Hardware interfaces
* Music-production utilities
* Experimental audio systems
* Research software

The organization provides a home for projects that require serious audio engineering while remaining connected to the broader creative and technological vision of CTP.

---

## Linux First

FIN-DSP is **Linux-first**.

Our development environment is centered on GNU/Linux and, where appropriate, **CTP-EOS Linux**.

Linux provides the foundation for our work because it gives developers direct access to the operating system, audio stack, hardware, development tools, and open-source ecosystem required for serious experimentation.

Projects may eventually support additional platforms, but Linux remains the primary development environment and engineering reference platform.

---

## CTP-EOS

FIN-DSP is closely connected to **CTP-EOS Linux**, the operating-system platform developed within the CTP ecosystem.

CTP-EOS provides the computing environment for experimentation, development, music production, audio engineering, and future CTP technologies.

FIN-DSP contributes the audio and DSP layer of that ecosystem.

**CTP-EOS is the operating environment.
FIN-DSP is the audio engineering organization.**

---

## Audio Philosophy

We believe computer audio should be more than another layer of conventional commercial software.

The computer is an instrument.

The operating system is part of the instrument.

The DSP architecture is part of the instrument.

The interface between human intention, computation, sound, and perception is itself an area worthy of exploration.

FIN-DSP therefore supports both conventional engineering and experimental approaches to sound.

We are interested in what happens when developers stop treating audio software as merely another category of productivity software and begin treating it as a serious creative and technological medium.
